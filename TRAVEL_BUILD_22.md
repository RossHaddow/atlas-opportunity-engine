# Atlas Travel Build 22 — Flexible Travel Timing Explorer

Build 22 adds flexible month-window searches instead of requiring fixed dates. It ranks seasonality by weather, daylight, crowd pressure, and value, clearly labels peak-season tradeoffs, and keeps results honest as planning estimates until live prices are connected.

Europe/UK profiles also rank meetup hubs for an MSP-origin couple and a Scotland-origin traveler. The initial hubs are Amsterdam, London, Copenhagen, and Glasgow/Edinburgh.
